Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This website uses no analytics, marketing or tracking cookies. There is no Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar tool active. The only technical storage used is strictly necessary for the site to function and does not require consent under the ePrivacy Directive/GDPR.
